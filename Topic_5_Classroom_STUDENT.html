Andina Energy Group: Restructuring the Employee Experience Function

When Andina Energy Group announced that its Employee Experience function would be restructured, reactions across the business were mixed. The decision had been under review for several months, but the timeline was accelerated after a wave of resignations was reported in the Bogotá office.

Under the new structure, three regional roles are being consolidated into a single LatAm-wide position. Employees affected by the change were notified individually before the broader announcement was made — a sequencing choice that is widely regarded as best practice in change management, since trust is more easily damaged than rebuilt.

Not everyone was convinced the transition had been handled well. Concerns were raised in an internal survey, where several respondents noted that the reasoning behind the restructuring had not been clearly explained. It was later acknowledged by leadership that communication could have been improved, and a follow-up town hall has now been scheduled.

The DEI implications of the restructuring are also being closely watched. Because two of the three consolidated roles were held by women, questions have been asked about whether diversity commitments were adequately considered during planning. An external audit is expected to be completed by the end of the quarter, and its findings will be shared once the review has been finalized.

For now, the transition is being framed internally as a necessary step toward a leaner employee experience strategy — though whether that framing will be accepted widely remains to be seen.

Examples: "are being consolidated," "are also being closely watched," "is being framed internally." The continuous form shows the action is still happening now, not finished — fitting, since the restructuring and the review are both still in progress.
